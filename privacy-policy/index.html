This policy explains what information Smart Calculator Hub ("the app", "we") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information you enter in the app

Almost everything you type into the app — salaries, budgets, statistics datasets, notes, health inputs, designs, recipes and other calculator values — is processed and stored only on your device. We do not upload this data to our servers, and it is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

Reports you export (PDF, Excel, Word, images) are created on your device and saved or shared only where you choose.

3. Google Sign-In (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your email address through Firebase Authentication. We use it only to link your Premium subscription to your account and to limit the free tier to one account per device. For this we store, in Google Cloud Firestore:

  • your email address and whether it currently has an active subscription, and
  • your device's Android ID together with the first email that signed in on that device.

You can use the app without signing in.

5. Features that send text to online services

Some features send only the text you enter to an online service to work:

  • Plagiarism checker — sends phrases from your text to the DuckDuckGo search engine to look for matching web pages.
  • Culinary calculator — sends the dish name you search to recipe services (TheMealDB, Edamam, Spoonacular, API Ninjas).
  • Canvas Craft stock photos — sends your search words to Pexels.
  • Translator and the paraphraser download language or AI models (from Google and Hugging Face); translation and paraphrasing then run on your device.

Do not enter sensitive personal information into these features.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • On-device data stays until you clear app data or uninstall the app.
  • To delete your sign-in record (email, subscription status and device record) from our Firestore database, email us at the contact address above from the email you signed in with. We will delete it within 30 days.
  • Data held by third-party services is kept according to their own policies.

8. Your choices

  • Personalised ads: reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads, and change your consent choices from the consent message where it is shown.
  • Ad-free: a Premium subscription removes ads.
  • Permissions: manage them in Android Settings → Apps → Smart Calculator Hub → Permissions.

9. Security

We use Google's secure services (Firebase, Google Play) to handle the small amount of data we store. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always available at this page, with the effective date shown at the top.
